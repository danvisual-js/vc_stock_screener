What it does

Three things, done calmly.

Most finance apps try to keep you trading. This one tries to keep you informed.

01 · Concierge

Type a question the way you would ask a friend who follows the market. It checks live quotes, recent headlines and the web, then answers with sources.

The whole week at a glance

Every ticker in a box with its estimates, grouped before the open and after the close, and highlighted when it is on your list. Tap one to open its analysis.

Why this exists

I'm a product designer, not a Wall Street person. I wanted one calm place that tells me what moved, why, and what is next.

Every finance app I tried either buried me in jargon or nudged me to trade. I did not need another way to gamble. I needed to understand my own watchlist in the five minutes I had with my morning coffee.

So I started building one on nights and weekends, with my own time and money. It has a screener, health flags that explain what to be careful about, an earnings calendar, and an AI concierge that talks like a person.

It is early, and the next features come from the people who use it. If that sounds like you, join the beta and tell me what is missing.

Questions

The honest answers.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is an educational tool. It never tells you to buy, sell or hold, and AI answers can be wrong or out of date, so check the sources. Talk to a licensed adviser for decisions about your money.

Where does the data come from?

Prices, headlines and earnings dates come from public market-data providers, and the Concierge can search the web. Estimates and dates can change.

What do you do with my email?

It is used only for beta access and the daily brief if you ask for it. It is never sold. The daily brief needs a confirmation click, and every email has a one-click unsubscribe.

Why is there a daily limit on AI answers?

Each AI answer costs money to run. Limits stop abuse and keep the free tier sustainable. Beta testers and supporters get bigger allowances.

Is it open source?

Not right now. The code stays private while the product is young.
